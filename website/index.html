§ 01 Why this became urgent in 2026

The security questionnaire grew an AI section. Now it decides your deals.

Enterprise procurement used to ask about SOC 2 and encryption. In the second half of 2026, effectively every serious vendor questionnaire also asks how you govern AI — which systems you use, what data they touch, who approved them, and what evidence you have.

Policy documents no longer pass. Buyers want management-system artifacts: a maintained register, dated approvals, and a review trail. Vendors who send a PDF policy fail the AI module and stall in procurement while a competitor answers in one line.

Scale Between 45% and 66% of the workforce uses AI tools their employer hasn't sanctioned. The average company runs 14 AI tools; IT knows about 4–5.1,2
Cost IBM puts the additional breach cost attributable to unsanctioned AI at $670,000 per incident.2
Law EU AI Act Article 50 transparency duties became binding 2 August 2026. They were not part of the delay that moved high-risk obligations to December 2027 — a distinction most teams have missed.3
Standard ISO/IEC 42001 is shifting from differentiator to qualification requirement in financial services, healthcare and public-sector procurement.4

§ 03 How it works

Four stages, in order. Everything downstream depends on the register being real.

Compliance platforms ask you to type in your AI inventory, then treat what you typed as fact. Provenant starts from observed evidence — the OAuth grants, admin consents and spend records that prove which systems actually hold your data.

STAGE 01

Discover

Read-only connections to your identity provider and spend systems surface every AI service holding a live grant against company data.

  • Google Workspace · Entra ID · Okta
  • Third-party OAuth grants + scopes
  • Card and AP spend reconciliation
  • Optional browser signal for web-only tools
STAGE 02

Classify

Each system is scored on the data classes its scopes actually expose, then mapped to your obligations under each framework.

  • Data class inference from granted scopes
  • Provider vs. deployer role (EU AI Act)
  • Article 50 transparency applicability
  • Sub-processor and training-on-your-data flags
STAGE 03

Govern

Turn the register into a running management system, with owners, approvals and a dated review trail auditors accept.

  • Request → review → approve workflow
  • Named system owners and review cadence
  • AI use policy + employee attestation
  • ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST AI RMF control mapping
STAGE 04

Prove

Export the evidence buyers ask for — or send them a link and skip the spreadsheet entirely.

  • Trust Pack evidence bundle
  • AI-CAIQ and SIG AI module autofill
  • Public AI trust page
  • Read-only auditor access
§ 04 The Trust Pack

Answers with citations attached, not adjectives.

The reason AI questionnaires stall deals is that the answers are assertions. Provenant answers from the register, and every answer carries a pointer to the record that backs it — so the reviewer on the other side stops asking follow-ups.

One customer-facing link replaces the spreadsheet round-trip. When your register changes, the answers change with it.
